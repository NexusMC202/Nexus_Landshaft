## Tectonic

Nexus Landscape 0.8 adapts density-function and noise data from Tectonic
3.0.26 by Apollo and contributors:

https://github.com/Apollounknowndev/tectonic

The data was renamed into the `nexus_landscape` namespace and the optional
configuration indirection was resolved to literal data-pack functions so Nexus
Landscape does not require Tectonic or Lithostitched at runtime.

## TerraFirmaCraft study

TerraFirmaCraft 4.2.6 was inspected only to understand high-level physical
ideas such as coastal moderation, elevation cooling, inland drying and river
humidity. No TerraFirmaCraft source code or assets are included in Nexus
Landscape.
